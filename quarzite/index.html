    Welcome to the webpage dedicated to my original character/sona, Quarzite. Here you’ll find more information about them, along with art and other commissions I’ve collected over the years.

    I don’t really care what pronouns you use for them. Their design is purposefully androgynous, aside from the clothing.

    They have a wolf/jellyfish-style haircut: white hair with lavender shading. Prominent black, devil-like horns with cross markings.

    Outfit: black-and-white oversized hoodie, thigh-high socks, winged sneakers.

    I honestly never thought too deeply about it, since the character is kind of just me.

    If you like my character and feel like drawing them, go for it! You can send it to contact@micr.dev.
